## Ingredients

- 120g golden raisins
- 60ml sweet dessert wine (or apple juice)
- 180g golden granulated caster sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 120ml extra virgin olive oil
- 350g plain flour, sifted
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp baking soda/ bicarbonate of soda (bread soda)
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 lemon, zest only
- 500g cooking apples, peeled and diced small

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 180ºC/fan 160ºC. Line a 23cm spring form tin with parchment.
2. Place the raisins in a bowl and add the sweet wine, soak for 30 minutes.
3. Add the sugar to a mixing bowl, add the eggs and whisk until light and fluffy.
4. Warm the olive oil and slowly whisk in with the eggs and sugar.
5. Place the flour, cinnamon, ginger, bicarbonate of soda, salt and baking powder into a bowl. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the oil and egg mixture, folding in with a metal spoon.
6. Mix thoroughly. Add in the lemon zest and diced apples into the mixture.
7. Remove the raisins from the wine and add them to the mixture. Mix the mixture very thoroughly. The mixture should be stiff at this stage, spoon into the prepared cake tin. Bake for at least one hour, until a skewer placed in the middle comes out dry. Remove the cake from the pan and allow to cool on a wire rack.
