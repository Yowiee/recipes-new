## Ingredients

- 100g unsalted butter
- 150g medjool dates, pitted and finely chopped
- 100ml milk
- 200g rolled oats
- 4 large ripe bananas, peeled
- 60g soured cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 3 tbsp maple syrup

#### Crumble topping
- 1 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp raw sugar

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and line a 12 hole muffin tin with muffin cases.
2. Add the butter to a small saucepan over a medium heat, and let it melt. Once melted, increase the heat to high and stir the butter regularly as it starts to brown. Once it begins to smell nutty, remove from the heat, pour into a large mixing bowl and leave aside to cool.
3. Return the pan to a medium heat with the milk and the chopped medjool dates. Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring often, or until the dates have softened completely. Leave aside to cool.
With an electric mixer, beat the peanut butter, melted butter and vanilla until until combined. Beat in the sugar and honey, then beat in the eggs, one at a time.
4. Meanwhile, blend your oats in a high speed blender until you have a fine powder – this is your oat flour.
5. Mash the bananas and browned butter in a bowl until smooth. Stir in the soured cream, vanilla extract, eggs and date mixture. Now mix in the oat flour, bicarbonate of soda, baking powder, cinnamon and a pinch of salt until well combined. Leave for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, mix your crumble topping in another bowl.
6. Divide the batter between the muffin cases. Drizzle the tahini over the top of each one and use a skewer to swirl into the batter. Sprinkle the crumble topping over the muffins then put into the oven to bake for 25-30 minutes, or until golden brown and a skewer inserted into the muffins comes out clean.
7. Remove from the oven. Drizzle the maple syrup evenly across the muffins then leave aside to cool in the tin.
