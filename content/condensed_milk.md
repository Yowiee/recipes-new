## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole milk powder
- 1/2 cup castor or icing sugar
- 40g butter (melted or soft)
- 1/4 cup boiling water, add 1-2 tbsp more if required to get a thick consistency
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Place water, vanilla, sugar & butter in a blender, and swirl by hand to mix
2. Add milk powder and blend for 20 seconds
3. Add a tablespoon more water if mixture is too thick to pour
4. Pour into a sterilised jar and refridgerate for up to 5 days
