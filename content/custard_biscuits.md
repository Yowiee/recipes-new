## Ingredients

- 250g butter, softened
- 75g (1/2 cup) icing sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 260g (1 3/4 cups) plain flour
- 65g (1/2 cup) custard powder

## Directions
1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Line 2 baking trays with baking paper. Use a hand mixer to beat butter, sugar and vanilla until well combined.
2. Add flour and custard powder, and use a wooden spoon to mix until just combined.
3. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface and lightly knead until the mixture comes together. It goes from crumbly to silky, then stop.
4. Roll heaped teaspoonfuls of mixture into small balls. Place onto prepared baking trays. Press with a floured fork to flatten slightly. Bake for 12 minutes or until light golden. Allow to stand on trays for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool. Repeat with remaining biscuit mixture.
