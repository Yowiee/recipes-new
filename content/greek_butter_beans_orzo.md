## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion chopped
- 3 cloves garlic grated
- 150 g orzo
- 2 cans butter beans, drained and rinsed
- 750 ml vegetable stock, reduced-salt
- 220 g spinach or Swiss chard, or kale
- 0.5 lemon zest and juice
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill chopped – substitute basil or parsley
- 115 g crumbled feta
- 0.5 teaspoon salt 

## Directions

1. Sauté the onion: Heat 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add 1 medium onion (chopped) and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft.
2. Toast the orzo: Add 3 cloves garlic (grated) and 150 g orzo. Stir for 1–2 minutes, until the garlic smells fragrant and the orzo is lightly toasted.
3. Simmer the beans and orzo: Add 2 cans butter beans (rinsed), 750 ml vegetable broth, 0.5 teaspoon salt, and black pepper.
4. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the orzo is almost tender and the broth looks creamy. Add a splash of hot water or broth if it gets too dry.
5. Add the greens and finish: Stir in 220 g spinach and cook until wilted, about 1–2 minutes. If using kale or Swiss chard, add them 5 minutes earlier.
6. Turn off the heat and stir in the the zest and juice of 0.5 lemon, 2 tablespoons fresh dill, and 115 g crumbled feta.
7. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil if you like, and serve warm.
