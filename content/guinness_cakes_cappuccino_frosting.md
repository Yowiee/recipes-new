## Ingredients

#### For the cake:
- 1 cup Guinness (any other dark will be fine, too)
- 250g unsalted butter
- 75g cocoa powder, Dutch processed  
- 300g caster sugar
- 150mL sour cream
- 2 eggs
- 2½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 275g plain flour
- 2½ teaspoons bicarbonate of soda 

#### For the icing:
- 2 tablespoons instant coffee powder
- 125g cream cheese, softened  
- 125g salted butter, room temperature  
- 2½ cups icing mixture
- 1 teaspoon vanilla paste
- Cocoa powder, dusted, to serve  

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C, fan forced. Grease two six-hole muffin pans, preferably silicone; the deepest ones you can find. Dust the moulds with cocoa to minimise sticking.
2. For the batter, pour the Guinness into a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan. Place over a medium heat, add the butter and stir until the butter has melted. Remove from heat, then whisk in the cocoa and sugar.
3. In a separate mixing bowl, whisk the sour cream until smooth. Add the eggs, one at a time, then the vanilla, still whisking until it's combined and smooth.
4. Pour the sour cream mixture into the Guinness mixture and whisk together. Whisk in the flour and bicarbonate soda. Mix until combined.
5. Spoon the batter into the moulds, around two-thirds full. Bake for 20 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean. This is a moist cake, better a bit undercooked than overcooked. Allow to cool completely in the mould, then pop out.
6. For the icing, place the coffee granules into a small bowl, add a tablespoon of boiling water, dissolve the coffee and mix into a wet paste. Add a touch more boiling water if needed.
7. Place the cream cheese into the bowl of a stand mixer, add the butter and vanilla, and beat to combine. Now beat in the icing sugar a cup at a time, until your frosting is light and of a pipeable consistency. Now beat in the coffee paste until it's all incorporated.
8. Generously top the cakes with a thick layer of icing. Dust with cocoa to serve.
