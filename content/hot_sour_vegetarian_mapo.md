## Ingredients

- 500g tender plain white tofu
- Cooking oil, for deep-frying, plus 3 tbsp extra for the wok
- 100g minced pork
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 2 tbsp Sichuan chilli bean paste
- 1 small red onion (about 50g), peeled and finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tbsp finely chopped garlic
- 1 tbsp finely chopped ginger
- 2 tbsp finely chopped red chilli
- 1 tbsp sambal oelek
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper, to taste
- 1 tsp potato starch mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
- 3 tbsp finely sliced spring onions, green parts only

## Directions

1. Cut the tofu into even, 1-1½cm-thick bite-size pieces, then gently pat dry with kitchen paper.
2. Heat oil for deep-frying to 180C. Working in batches, fry the tofu for about five minutes, until golden brown, then drain on kitchen paper. You can do this step a few hours in advance.
3. Put three tablespoons of oil in a seasoned wok on a high heat. Add the pork and stir-fry, breaking up the meat, then, when it turns pale, add the Shaoxing wine. When the meat is a little golden, turn down the heat to medium, add the chilli bean paste and stir until the oil reddens. Add the onion, fry until it smells delicious, then do the same with the garlic, ginger and chilli. Finally, stir in the sambal oelek. You should have a rubbly mass of utter deliciousness.
4. Pour in 250ml water and add a little white pepper to taste. Drop in the fried tofu, nudge the pieces into the sauce, then return to a boil and simmer for a minute or so, to “send the flavours in”. Give the starch mixture a stir and, in a couple of stages, add just enough to thicken the sauce to a lazy gravy. Stir in the spring onion greens and serve.
