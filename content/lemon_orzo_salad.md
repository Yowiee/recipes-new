## Ingredients

- 200 g orzo uncooked
- 220 g frozen peas
- 200 g cucumber diced or cut into discs
- 150 g feta cheese crumbled
- 1 handful fresh parsley chopped
- 1 handful fresh dill sub basil or cilantro
- 40 g chopped toasted nuts optional (such as pistachios, sliced almonds, or walnuts)

#### For the Lemon Dressing
- 60 ml extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon grated lemon zest from about 1 large lemon
- 60 ml fresh lemon juice
- 20 ml honey
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon salt plus black pepper to taste

## Directions

1. Cook the orzo and peas: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add 200 g orzo and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. During the last 2 minutes of cooking, stir in 220 g frozen peas. Drain well, but don't rinse.
2. Make the dressing: While the orzo cooks, whisk together 60 ml extra virgin olive oil, 1 tablespoon grated lemon zest, 60 ml fresh lemon juice, 20 ml honey, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, 0.75 teaspoon salt, and black pepper in a large bowl.
3. Stir in 150 g feta cheese (crumbled).
4. Toss and cool: Add the hot orzo and peas to the bowl and toss well. The warm pasta will soften some of the feta. Let the salad cool for 5 minutes.
5. Finish the salad: Add 200 g cucumber, 1 handful fresh parsley, and 1 handful fresh dill, then toss gently.
6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed, then mix in 40 g chopped toasted nuts just before serving.
