## Ingredients

- 85g dried apricots
- 85g glacé cherries 
- 85g sultanas
- 2 tablespoons (40mL) sherry
- 225g plain flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon table salt
- 125g unsalted butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (110g) caster sugar
- 2 eggs, room temperature
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest, finely grated
- 1/2 cup (125mL) milk, room temperature 

## Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 140ºC, fan-forced. Line an 18cm round cake tin with 4 layers of baking paper on the base and the side.
2. Finely dice the apricots and cherries so they are the same size as the sultanas. Place in a bowl and add the sultanas.
3. Heat the sherry in a small bowl in the microwave for 30 seconds on high, then pour over the fruit and mix well to combine. Set aside.
4. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt into a large bowl and set aside.
5. In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, place the butter and sugar and beat until very pale, light, and creamy, scraping down the side of the bowl as needed.
6. Add the eggs one at a time, until fully combined into the batter, followed by the vanilla and lemon zest.
7. In batches, slowly add one third of the flour mixture at a time into the batter, alternating with the milk, until fully combined. Using a rubber spatula, fold in the dried fruit and sherry until evenly distributed through the batter.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared cake tin and gently spread the mixture out evenly, smoothing the surface with an offset spatula.
9. Bake for 1 hour and 30-35 minutes, or until lightly golden and cooked when tested with a skewer.
10. Allow the fruit cake to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then carefully turn out onto a tea towel-lined wire rack, removing the baking paper. Place the cake onto a plate for serving.
