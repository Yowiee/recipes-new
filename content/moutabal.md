## Ingredients

#### Moutabal
- 2 large eggplants
- 2 tablespoons tahini
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons yoghurt
- 1 garlic clove, minced

#### Topping
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon pine nuts
- 250g beef mince
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- ¾ teaspoon Lebanese baharat spice mix
- 1 teaspoon pomegranate molasses

#### To serve
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- Pomegranate seeds
- Fresh parsley, roughly chopped
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Lebanese pita bread, to serve

## Directions

1. Poke the eggplants all over using a skewer. Place over an open flame and cook, turning frequently, until soft and the skin is charred. This usually takes about 10 minutes. This can also be done in an oven by placing the eggplants on a lined baking tray and roasting for about 40 minutes.
2. Remove the charred skin and place the flesh of the eggplant into a bowl. Using a mortar and pestle, mash the garlic with the salt. Add the eggplant and mash until you have a coarse, slightly chunky texture. You can use a food processor, but hand-mashing gives the moutabal a better texture.
3. Add your tahini, lemon juice and yoghurt and garlic. Mix well. Place in an airtight container and put in the fridge until ready to serve.
4. To prepare the topping, heat olive oil in a frypan. Add pine nuts and toast until they just turn a golden brown. Do not leave unattended because this will only take a minute. Remove from pan and set aside.
5. Using the same pan, cook mince, stir to brown and remove large lumps of meat. Add salt, baharat & pomegranate molasses. Stir through & remove from heat once all meat is cooked through. Return pine nuts to pan. Mix well.
6. To serve, generously spread moutabal all over a large plate. Top the moutabal with spoonfuls of the spiced meat. Add some diced onion and tomatoes, freshly chopped parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.
7. Serve with Lebanese pita bread.
