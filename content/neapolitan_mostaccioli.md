## Ingredients

- 250g plain flour
- 75g ground almonds
- 25g cocoa powder
- Finely grated zest of 1 unwaxed orange
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/1 pinch salt
- 90g caster sugar
- 1 1/2 tbsp honey, or golden syrup or mostocotto
- 40ml orange juice
- 150g dark chocolate

## Directions
1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, ground almonds, cocoa powder, orange zest, spices, baking powder and salt. In a small pan, heat 100ml water with the sugar, honey and orange juice, stirring until the sugar dissolves completely.
2. Add the wet ingredients to the dry, then mix to a soft, pliable dough, Bring the dough into a ball, cover and set aside to rest for 30 minutes.
3. Working on a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough until it’s 6-8mm thick, then, using a knife or suitable cutter, cut it into roughly 8cm-long diamonds. Transfer these to an oven tray lined with greaseproof paper, then bake in a 180°C (160°C fan) for 12-14 minutes, until the biscuits are still a bit soft and light in colour – they must not darken. Transfer the mostaccioli to a rack and leave to cool completely.
4. Melt the chocolate in a bain-marie bowl balanced over a pan of simmering water (or in the microwave). Using two forks, dip and roll the biscuits one by one in the melted chocolate until they are completely covered, then return to the rack (this time set over a sheet of baking paper to catch any drips) and leave to cool and set.
