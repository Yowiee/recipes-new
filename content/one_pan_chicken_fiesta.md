## Ingredients

- 1.2kg skinless and boneless chicken thighs
- ½ cup olive oil
- ¼ teaspoon white pepper
- ½ tablespoon black pepper
- 1½ tablespoons salt
- 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
- 6 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
- 1 roughly chopped red capsicum
- 1 roughly chopped red onion
- ½ cup Worcestershire sauce
- 1 large bunch of coriander
- 2 fresh limes
- 1 cup white basmati rice 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 200°C
2. Place chicken thighs into a large baking dish, add olive oil, salt, white and black pepper, paprika, garlic cloves, capsicum, onion and Worcestershire sauce to the chicken.
3. Chop and add the bottom half of the coriander bunch (the stems and some leaves).
4. Add the juice and zest of one lime.
5. Mix well and place uncovered in the oven.
6. Take out after 40 minutes, add rice, turn chicken and veg, and return to the oven. If the liquid level in the tray looks low, add 1-2 cups of water (this can vary depending on moisture content in the fresh chicken).
7. After another 30 minutes, remove from the oven and rest for 5-10 minutes before serving.
8. Serve with fresh lime juice and zest, and roughly chopped coriander over the top.
