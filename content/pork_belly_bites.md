## Ingredients

- 1kg boneless pork belly
- sea salt

#### Sauce
- 250ml fresh orange juice
- 3cm knob ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
- 2 wholes star anise
- 100g caster sugar
- 2 tbs light soy sauce

## Directions

Dry the pork: Place the pork belly in a shallow dish uncovered in the fridge for at least 8 hours or overnight. Cut into 4cm pieces.
Make the sauce: Combine your all sauce ingredients in a saucepan, simmer for 10-15 minutes until reduced and thick.
Season: Spray the pork liberally with oil and sprinkle with salt.
Air fry: Cook half the pork in the air fryer at 200°C for 15 minutes until golden and crisp. Repeat with the remaining pork and serve with the sauce
