## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati rice
- 2 cinnamon stick
- 1 pinch saffron (sub 1/4 tsp tumeric & paprika)
- 1 lemon, juice and zest
- 3 tbsp currants
- 3 tbsp pine nuts
- 4 boneless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp Za’atar
- 1/2 bunch parsley
- 2 tsp softened butter
- salt to taste

## Directions

1. Add the rice to a rice cooker bowl, rinse twice under cold water and drain well. Add half a cup of fresh water to the rice cooker with ¼ tsp of salt, a pinch of saffron, currants, a cinnamon stick and the juice of half a lemon. 
2. Place the chicken thighs on a tray and liberally season with salt, pepper, and Za’atar. Place on top of the rice in a single layer.
3. Close the lid to the rice cooker and place on a standard rice cycle. In the meantime, finely slice the parsley leaves and set aside.
4. Once cooked, remove the chicken and cinnamon from the cooker. Add a small handful of pine nuts, the zest of half a lemon, butter and half of the finely sliced parsley. Stir it through the rice, then serve with the chicken on the side. Garnish with the remaining sliced parsley and enjoy.

#### Note:
- If you don't have a rice cooker use a large saucepan, with a lid, on medium-low
- To check that your chicken is cooked, poke it with a digital thermometer and it should be at 62°C.
