## Ingredients

- 2 corn cobs
- 5 eggs
- 1 cup (150g) self-raising flour
- 2 teaspoons taco seasoning, plus ½ teaspoon extra to serve
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, plus extra for drizzling
- 400g (2 medium zucchini), coarsely grated
- 4 spring onions, thinly sliced, white and green parts kept separate
- 140g smoked cheddar, coarsely grated (regular cheddar works too)
- 1 tablespoon Kewpie mayonnaise, plus ¼ cup extra to serve
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped pickled jalapeños, plus extra to serve (optional)
- Lime wedges and Grana Padano, parmesan or pecorino, finely grated, to serve

## Directions

1. For the oven, preheat to 180°C. Grease and line a 20cm x 20cm baking tin with baking paper. You can also use a 20cm x 30cm tin if you prefer a thinner slice. For the air fryer, preheat to 180°C. Line the air-fryer basket with a 20cm square air-fryer liner. You'll need an air fryer large enough to fit it comfortably.
2. Drizzle the corn with a little extra oil and massage it over the kernels. Heat a large frying pan over high heat, add the corn and cook, turning occasionally, for 6–8 minutes or until charred all over. Set aside until cool enough to handle, then use a sharp knife to slice the kernels from the cobs.
3. Whisk the eggs in a large bowl. Add the flour and taco seasoning and whisk until just combined. Fold through the zucchini, most of the smoked cheddar, the white parts of the spring onions, vegetable oil, mayonnaise, jalapeños (if using) and half the charred corn. Season with black pepper.
4. Spoon the mixture into the prepared tin or air-fryer liner and smooth the top. Scatter over the remaining corn and remaining smoked cheddar. (this is how we get the gnarly corn bits by having some on top!)
5. Bake at 180°C, for 40–45 minutes in an oven or 30-35 minutes in an air fryer, or until deeply golden on top and just firm in the centre. Stand for 10 minutes to cool slightly.
6. Combine the extra mayonnaise and taco seasoning in a small bowl. Spoon over the top and use the back of a spoon to roughly smear it across the slice. Scatter with the green spring onion and extra jalapeños, then finely grate over plenty of Grana Padano, parmesan or pecorino. Slice and serve.
