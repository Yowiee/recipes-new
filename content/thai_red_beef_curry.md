## Ingredients

- 3 1/2 Tbsp AYAM Thai Red Curry Paste
- 500 g beef fillet, diced into 2cm pieces
- 270 ml can AYAM Coconut Cream
- 1 1/2 cup water
- 100 g cabbage leaves, chopped into small pieces
- 2 kaffir lime leaves, finely sliced
- Basil leaves, to garnish

## Directions

1. Sizzle: Heat a wok or pan over medium-high heat.
2. Brown: Add curry paste and beef, stir-fry until beef is browned and coated in paste. Stir in coconut cream, water and bring to a boil.
3. Simmer: Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Finish: Add cabbage and kaffir lime leaves, cook for a further 5 minutes until the beef is tender.
5. Serve: Garnish with basil leaves. Serve with steamed rice.
